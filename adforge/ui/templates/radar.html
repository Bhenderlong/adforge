Conversation radar

Threads on Reddit and Discord where your product is a real answer.

How this works. Keyword prefilter over recent threads, then a model scores whether the product genuinely answers the question. Anything above the target's threshold can be drafted a reply. Every reply carries a disclosure and requires your approval before it is sent.

Replies that pose as an unaffiliated happy user are not generated, and the policy check blocks them if a model produces one anyway. Undisclosed endorsements by a company's own people breach the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255), and on Reddit they escalate to a sitewide domain ban that would stop anyone linking your site anywhere on the platform. Disclosed, genuinely useful replies survive; astroturf gets removed.

Scanning everything. On Reddit set the target to all — that searches the entire site for your keywords through the official search API. Threads found that way are never given a link, because they sit in communities whose self-promotion rules you have not read. Add that subreddit as its own target once you have read them.

Discord cannot be scanned globally, by anyone. There is no directory and no cross-server search — a bot only sees servers it has been invited to. Target * scans every channel your bot can currently read; to widen it, join the server and invite the bot. The only thing that could read all of Discord is a self-bot on a user account, which its ToS forbids and which gets the account terminated.
